# w_loading credits

Every image, recording and typeface this resource ships, with where it came from and the licence it is used under. Nothing here was generated by AI. The plate captions on the loading screen show each photographer and year. The full records are listed below.

## Survey plates (`html/img/plates/`)

Each plate was cropped inside the print, so no mount board, handwritten captions or publisher imprints remain. All seven were then graded with one recipe: automatic levels and exposure, a gentle S-curve, and a warm albumen monochrome ramp (shadows `#14110D`, mids `#6E6557`, highlights `#E4DCCB`). Each was saved as a 1920x1080 progressive JPEG at q80 (`hanging_rock.jpg` at q78, the step that keeps it under 500 KB). That tone stays in the files only: the loading screen draws every plate black and white (`filter: grayscale(1)` on `.plate` in `html/loading.css`, as the launcher's plates), so no cream reaches the screen. Their captions are the `screen.plates.<id>.*` keys in `locales/`.

## Other

- `html/img/pm-grain.png`: the grain tile, generated procedurally by `w_pausemenu/web/tools/gen-textures.mjs`. It is our own work.
- **Code:** everything in `html/` was written for this resource. The event handling follows the loadscreen events of the Cfx runtime. The idea of an in-browser event replay (`?dev=1`) comes from D4isDAVID/loadscreen (MIT, https://github.com/D4isDAVID/loadscreen), but none of its code is copied, so there is no `THIRD_PARTY.md`.

## Rockstar Games

Rockstar's Cfx.re Platform License Agreement, section 2.3, requires user-facing server material to carry a contact email and a line substantially similar to "WRAITH CORE IS NOT APPROVED, SPONSORED, OR ENDORSED BY ROCKSTAR GAMES." The loading screen shows that line in small type along its bottom edge (`screen.legal`), plus the contact address when `Config.ContactEmail` is set. The page uses no Rockstar art, logos or screenshots.
